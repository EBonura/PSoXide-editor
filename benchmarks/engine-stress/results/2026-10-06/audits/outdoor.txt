## Sealing

LEAKS. A 4-point path escapes to the void. The engine cannot compute visibility through a leak, so the whole map falls back to drawing everything.
  path starts at authored [0, 16, -4096] (engine [0, 1, -256]), which is the OCCUPANT the flood starts from, normally the player.
  Check that point is inside your geometry first. A player left at another level's coordinates reports every map as leaking, however well sealed it is.
